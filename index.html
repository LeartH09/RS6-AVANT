Overview

Luxury wagon. RS attitude.

The 2024 Audi RS 6 Avant performance is a high-performance luxury wagon that combines supercar-level speed with everyday practicality. It brings 5 seats, quattro all-wheel drive, aggressive RS styling, premium interior quality, and genuine wagon usability into one low, wide, grey package.

This fan-made concept leans into a Nardo Gray and Daytona Gray mood: matte black surfaces, brushed silver edges, red RS energy, and a design language built around speed without giving up utility.

Performance

Twin-turbo V8 force, tuned for daily use.

The RS 6 Avant performance turns the long-roof formula into something brutally quick, with a 4.0L mild-hybrid V8, quattro grip, RS drive modes, and chassis hardware built to stay composed under pressure.

Engine bay

4.0L TFSI V8

A twin-turbo mild-hybrid V8 sits at the center of the RS 6 Avant performance formula, delivering 621 hp through an 8-speed Tiptronic automatic and quattro all-wheel drive.
